🏭 MRF Facility Locations
How to mark an MRF facility:
  1. Stand at the actual MRF facility location
  2. Tap My Location — your GPS position appears as a pulsing blue dot
  3. Enter the facility Name (required)
  4. Tap Save Facility Location
  5. Repeat for each MRF facility you visit
You can also click the map manually if you can't be there in person. Drag the pin to fine-tune.
