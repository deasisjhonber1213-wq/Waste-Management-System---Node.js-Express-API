Terms & Privacy

1. Using This App

This portal is provided by the Jose Dalman Waste Management office so residents can view truck locations, check pickup schedules, and report waste-collection issues in their community.

2. Reports You Submit

Reports (with photos and location) that you submit are shared with barangay/city waste management staff for the purpose of resolving the issue you reported.

3. Fair Use

Please avoid submitting false reports or misusing the location-sharing feature. Accounts found abusing the system may be suspended.

4. Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated terms.

What We Collect

Your name, contact details, and — only when you choose to enable it — your live location while "Share" is turned on, plus the location and photo attached to any report you submit.

How It's Used

Location data is used only to show nearby collection trucks and to help drivers/staff locate reported issues. It is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Your Control

Location sharing can be turned off anytime with the Share button on the map. You can update your profile details at any time from My Profile.

Retention

Report history and location logs are kept only as long as needed for waste-management operations and record-keeping.
