📍 GPS Coordinates & Location Timestamps — What do they mean?

Each truck stores a latitude, longitude, and a location_updated_at timestamp. The coordinates pin the truck on the map below and on the resident's live tracking view. The timestamp tells you how fresh that position is — so you can tell whether a truck is actively moving right now or whether the dot on the map is hours old.

Coordinates are updated in two ways: (1) automatically every 15 seconds when a driver has GPS sharing turned on in the Driver Portal, or (2) manually via the "📍 Location" button on each truck card below.

● Green — updated < 1 hour ago (driver likely active)
● Amber — 1–12 hours ago
● Red — over 12 hours (position likely stale)
